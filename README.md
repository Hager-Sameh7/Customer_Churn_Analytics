# Customer Churn Analytics Hub | Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
The **Customer Churn Analytics Hub** is an interactive **Power BI** dashboard designed to analyze customer churn behavior and identify the key factors influencing customer retention. The dashboard provides executive-level insights into customer demographics, service usage, payment behavior, and financial performance to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Monitor overall customer churn performance.
- Identify the main drivers of customer churn.
- Analyze customer behavior across services and payment methods.
- Explore demographic and financial patterns.
- Provide actionable recommendations to improve customer retention.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 🏠 Home
A navigation page that provides quick access to all dashboard sections:
- Executive Overview
- Services & Support Analysis
- Financials & Demographics

---

### 📈 Executive Overview
Provides a high-level summary of business performance.

**KPIs**
- Total Customers
- Churn Rate
- Total Yearly Charges
- Total Admin Tickets

**Visualizations**
- Churn Rate by Internet Service
- Churn Rate by Payment Method
- Total Customers by Internet Service
- Churn Rate by Contract Type

**Key Insights**
- Fiber Optic customers experience the highest churn.
- Month-to-month contracts have significantly higher churn.
- Electronic Check customers are more likely to leave.

---

### 🎧 Services & Support Analysis
Analyzes how customer services impact retention.

**KPIs**
- Tech Support Tickets
- Streaming TV Adoption
- Monthly Charges
- Admin Tickets

**Visualizations**
- Churn Rate by Tech Support
- Churn Rate by Online Security
- Churn Rate by Internet Service
- Customers by Phone Service

**Business Insights**
- Customers without Tech Support have higher churn.
- Online Security adoption reduces churn.
- Phone Service represents the largest customer segment.
- Fiber Optic customers require additional retention strategies.

---

### 💰 Financials & Demographics
Explores financial metrics and customer demographics.

**KPIs**
- Total Charges
- Senior Citizens
- Streaming Movies Adoption
- Monthly Charges

**Visualizations**
- Churn Rate by Partner Status
- Customer Distribution by Partner
- Financial & Demographic Insights

**Recommendations**
- Encourage long-term contracts with promotional offers.
- Develop retention campaigns for customers without partners.
- Offer personalized plans for senior citizens.
- Reduce churn through loyalty and discount programs.

---

## 📌 Key Business Insights

- 📉 Overall churn rate is **26.58%**.
- 🌐 Fiber Optic users have the highest churn rate.
- 📅 Month-to-month contracts contribute the most to customer churn.
- 💳 Customers using Electronic Check exhibit higher churn behavior.
- 🤝 Customers without partners are more likely to churn.
- 🛡️ Tech Support and Online Security services significantly improve customer retention.
